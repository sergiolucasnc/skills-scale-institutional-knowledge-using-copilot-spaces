# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work closely with QA/Testing Specialists to ensure quality standards are met
- Collaborate with Technical Leads on architecture and design decisions
- Partner with Scrum Masters to resolve impediments and optimize workflow
- Receive acceptance criteria from Product Managers
- Implement security requirements defined by Security & Compliance Officers

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Stakeholders/Sponsors to align on business priorities
- Define acceptance criteria for Developers
- Work with Project Managers on timelines and resource planning
- Collaborate with QA/Testing Specialists on test strategies and acceptance criteria
- Engage Technical Leads on feasibility and architectural constraints

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with Scrum Masters to optimize delivery processes and ceremonies
- Escalate risks and blockers identified by team members to Stakeholders/Sponsors
- Collaborate with Technical Leads on technical dependency management
- Coordinate with QA/Testing Specialists on test timelines and quality gates
- Engage Security & Compliance Officers on compliance and security scheduling

---

## QA/Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Specialists validate quality, ensure acceptance criteria are met, and maintain test coverage standards throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated tests across all testing levels
- Perform exploratory testing to identify edge cases and potential issues
- Report defects with clear reproduction steps and severity levels
- Validate releases and ensure readiness for production deployment
- Maintain and enhance test automation frameworks
- Track test coverage metrics and quality indicators

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases that meet acceptance criteria
- Catch defects early in the development lifecycle
- Build team and stakeholder confidence in product readiness
- Reduce post-release incidents and support issues

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions during sprint planning
- QA reviews and updates in daily standups
- Defect reports and quality dashboards
- Pre-release checklists and sign-offs
- Collaboration on test scenarios and edge cases

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Developers on test-driven development practices and bug fixes
- Align with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and test scenarios
- Coordinate with Project Managers on test schedules and quality gates
- Collaborate with Technical Leads on test infrastructure and automation strategy
- Support Security & Compliance Officers with security testing requirements

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors provide business context, approve scope and resources, and own project success metrics. They represent business and customer interests.

### Responsibilities
- Define business needs and validate problem statements
- Approve project scope, budget, and resource allocation
- Make priority decisions and resolve scope trade-offs
- Validate outcomes and measure business impact
- Escalate organizational obstacles and resource constraints
- Serve as final authority on go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Ensure business value and ROI delivery
- Maintain alignment between project execution and business strategy
- Remove organizational barriers to project success
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business needs

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee and milestone approval meetings
- Executive status updates and business impact reviews
- Escalation paths for high-impact decisions
- Stakeholder briefings on progress and risks
- Budget and resource approval processes

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with Project Managers on escalations and strategic decisions
- Review outcomes validated by QA/Testing Specialists before approval
- Align with Product Managers on business priorities and success metrics
- Provide approval authority for Technical Leads on architectural decisions with business impact
- Engage with Security & Compliance Officers on compliance and risk tolerance

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate iterative delivery processes, remove impediments, and coach the team on agile practices and ceremonies.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standup, review, retrospective)
- Identify and help remove team impediments and blockers
- Coach team members on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain sprint velocity tracking and burndown visibility
- Foster psychological safety and collaborative team culture
- Track and facilitate resolution of action items from retrospectives

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and sustainable delivery pace
- Improve cross-functional collaboration and communication
- Continuously optimize delivery processes and ceremonies
- Build an adaptive, high-performing team

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremony facilitation and timeboxing
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Process coaching and impediment escalation
- Team morale and culture building
- Agile metrics and improvement tracking

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Project Managers to ensure ceremonies are effective and aligned with timelines
- Support Developers by removing impediments and optimizing workflow
- Facilitate communication between Developers and QA/Testing Specialists
- Coach all team members on collaboration and agile principles
- Coordinate with Technical Leads on technical impediment resolution
- Escalate organizational blockers to Project Managers and Stakeholders

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical strategy, design guidance, and ensure solutions align with architecture and scalability principles. They mentor developers and manage technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and architectural approaches
- Review code and architecture for quality, scalability, and maintainability
- Mentor Developers on technical best practices and standards
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Evaluate and recommend tools, technologies, and frameworks
- Ensure technical consistency across the codebase
- Lead design reviews and architectural discussions

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Reduce technical debt and improve code quality
- Share knowledge and grow technical capability across the team
- Minimize future rework and technical risks

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical mentoring and code review sessions
- Risk assessments and technical feasibility analysis
- Tool and technology evaluation discussions
- Technical documentation and decision logging

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guide Developers on architectural decisions and technical standards
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Work with Project Managers on technical dependency management
- Partner with QA/Testing Specialists on test automation architecture and strategy
- Ensure Security & Compliance Officers' requirements are incorporated into architecture
- Report technical risks to Project Managers and Stakeholders

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security and compliance requirements, manage security risks, and support incident response. They protect systems, data, and organizational compliance.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Participate in code reviews for security vulnerabilities
- Manage vulnerability response and remediation tracking
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Lead or support security incident response
- Maintain security documentation and compliance artifacts

### Goals
- Protect systems and customer data from security threats
- Reduce security incidents and vulnerabilities
- Maintain regulatory compliance and audit readiness
- Build security awareness and culture across teams

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements gathering and workshops
- Security code review participation
- Vulnerability and incident response communications
- Compliance audits and assessments
- Security training and awareness sessions
- Risk registers and mitigation tracking

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Developers to implement secure coding practices
- Guide Technical Leads on security architecture and design patterns
- Define security acceptance criteria with Product Managers
- Coordinate with QA/Testing Specialists on security testing and validation
- Escalate critical security risks to Project Managers and Stakeholders
- Advise Project Managers on security timelines and risk management
